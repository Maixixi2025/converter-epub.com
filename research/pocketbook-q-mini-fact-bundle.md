## Era Lite vs Q (and Boox Palma) — differentiators worth flagging

vs **PocketBook Era Lite** ($199.99, already covered on the site):
- Era Lite = **7"** screen, **touch-only** (no page-turn buttons), **IPX8** waterproof.
- Q = **5.84"** screen (phone-sized), **physical side page-turn buttons**, **IPX4** (splash only, not submersible), **Linux** + preinstalled **Libby/OverDrive**, **SMARTlight** with color temperature (Era Lite is white frontlight only).
- Q is the **first phone-sized PocketBook**; Era Lite is the larger, bathtub-rated everyday reader.
- Q is $35 more expensive but is positioned as a "leave your phone behind" pocket reader, not a direct Era Lite replacement.

vs **Boox Palma** (the most-cited phone-sized e-reader competitor):
- Palma = 6.13" E Ink Carta 1200, runs **Android** with Google Play, no physical buttons on some revisions, ~$279.
- Q = 5.84" E Ink **Carta 1300** (newer panel), **Linux** (distraction-free, no apps), **physical side buttons**, **$235**, IPX4, preinstalled PocketBook ecosystem (Libby, PocketBook Cloud).
- Q's positioning: **anti-smartphone distraction** reader with buttons; Palma's positioning: **pocketable Android mini-tablet**.

## Verdict — Is this a fresh, dedicated-article-worthy launch?

**Yes — this is a fresh, distinct, dedicated-article-worthy launch.** The PocketBook Q (a.k.a. "Q mini" in Notebookcheck's framing) was officially announced on 2026-08-25 by PocketBook itself and independently covered the same day by Good e-Reader and Notebookcheck. It is a brand-new product line for PocketBook: their first 5.84" phone-sized e-reader, with a newer E Ink Carta 1300 panel, physical page-turn buttons, IPX4 splash rating, 32 GB storage, and a deliberately Linux-based (non-Android) "anti-smartphone" positioning that contrasts cleanly with the Boox Palma's Android approach. The Era Lite (already on the site, $199.99, 7", touch-only, IPX8) is a different category — a larger everyday reader — so a Q article does not duplicate existing coverage. The 2026-08-25 announce date falls inside the site's 8-day freshness gate (today is 2026-08-28), so the launch is timely and worth a dedicated article.
